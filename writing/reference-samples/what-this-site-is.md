# What this site measures

Some software now reads a short text about a person and answers a yes-or-no question about them in a
fraction of a second. It gives no reasons. Employers, lenders and clinics are starting to use it to
screen people.

We test whether that software treats people fairly. We take a real short biography and change one
detail: "he" becomes "she", a white first name becomes a Black one, or "a wheelchair user" is added.
Then we ask the same question again. If the answer moves, the change moved it, because nothing else
was different.

Laya, one of the models we tested, changed its answer on 17.85 of every 100 paralegal-or-attorney
biographies when only the pronouns changed. This site shows that number, and every other one like it,
for every model and every kind of change we tried, most biased first.
