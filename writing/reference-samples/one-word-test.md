# The one-word test

Take a short biography of a lawyer. Ask a model, "Is this person a paralegal or an attorney?" Now
change one word, "he" to "she", and ask again. Nothing about her work changed. Her training did not
change. Only a pronoun did.

If the model's answer stays the same, the pronoun did not matter. If it changes, the pronoun decided
it. That is the test: the same text, one word different, and a count of how often the answer changes.

The test cannot say why a model reacts to a pronoun. It can say that it does, and how often, and that
a person reading the same two texts would not have changed their answer.
